# Choose Your Own Adventure

A console adventure in which a player creates a character, chooses a path, and meets whatever waits at its end.

## Language

### Journey

**Path**:
One of the routes the player may take from the mountain crossroads: North or South.
_Avoid_: Route, direction, trail

**Merchant**:
A travelling trader the player meets on their first journey down the South path, who offers magical goods for gold.
_Avoid_: Shopkeeper, vendor, trader, shop

**Merchant Encounter**:
The single meeting with the Merchant, in which the player may buy at most one magical item (Magical Armor or a Magical Weapon), plus any Healing Potions they can afford and carry.
_Avoid_: Shop visit, store, transaction

### Character

**Gold**:
The coins a character carries, given as a fixed purse when the character is created.
_Avoid_: Money, coins, currency, funds

**Character Summary**:
The full description of a character: who they are (name, race, occupation), their abilities and health, and their Inventory.
_Avoid_: Stats, character sheet, profile

**Inventory**:
Everything a character is carrying or wearing: their weapon, any Magical Armor, their Healing Potions, and their Gold. The player can check it at the crossroads or during combat.
_Avoid_: Bag, items, equipment, gear, belongings

**Maximum Health**:
The most health a character can have: their health when character creation ends, plus any health added by Magical Armor. Healing can never take a character above it.
_Avoid_: Max HP, full health, health cap

**Healing Potion**:
A single-use drink that gives a character back health lost in combat, up to their Maximum Health. It can only be drunk during combat.
_Avoid_: Potion (on its own), healing item, health pack, elixir

**Magical Armor**:
Enchanted armor sold by the Merchant that strengthens its wearer with extra health the moment it is put on.
_Avoid_: Armour, shield, protection, healing item

**Magical Weapon**:
An enchanted version of a character's occupation weapon that can deal more damage than the ordinary one.
_Avoid_: Upgraded weapon, enchanted weapon, new weapon
